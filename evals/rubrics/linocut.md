# Linocut relief print

The image should read as a hand-carved relief print: one or two inks on paper, with carved gouge marks making every tone.

## Limited ink
One or two inks on paper; paper is the lightest value. 1 = full colour or continuous tone. 3 = limited but with gradients or grey fills. 5 = strict one- or two-ink print.

## Gouge marks
Tone comes from carved marks (parallel cuts, contour cuts, stipple), denser where lighter. 1 = no carved marks. 3 = marks present but uniform or directionless. 5 = varied, confident cuts that describe form and light.

## Graphic contrast
Bold black shapes against carved whites, with a strong silhouette. 1 = grey and muddy. 3 = contrasty but busy. 5 = striking black-and-white design.

## Hand-made character
Uneven ink, ragged cut edges, chips and plate border. 1 = vector-clean. 3 = texture looks like a filter. 5 = convincingly hand carved and printed.

## Subject clarity
Subjects are immediately identifiable. 1 = unreadable. 3 = identifiable with effort. 5 = every subject reads at a glance.
