# Stained glass window

The image should read as a leaded stained-glass window: jewel-coloured glass panes joined by dark lead lines, lit from behind.

## Lead lines
Thick dark lead came divides every shape into glass pieces; the lines are continuous, slightly irregular, and plausible to build. 1 = no lead lines. 3 = lines present but uniform or arbitrary. 5 = convincing leading that follows and supports the design.

## Glass colour and texture
Saturated panes with internal variation (streaks, seeds, mottling) that look backlit. 1 = flat or dull colour. 3 = colourful but flat. 5 = glowing, textured glass.

## Pane design
Regions are cut into sensible panes (no hairline slivers), with sky and ground split into pieces. 1 = regions are single huge panes or noise. 3 = reasonable but mechanical (a plain grid or Voronoi look). 5 = panes follow forms like a real glazier's cartoon.

## Window structure
Reads as a window: border, frame, and optional saddle bars. 1 = just a picture. 3 = some framing. 5 = convincing window object.

## Subject clarity
Subjects are immediately identifiable through the leading. 1 = unreadable. 3 = identifiable with effort. 5 = everything reads at a glance.
